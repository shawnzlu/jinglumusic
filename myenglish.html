Vocal Lesson

Jane Lu is now offering lessons in proper vocal techniques of various musical styles. Students at all levels are welcome! Jane Lu is willing to work with students towards a goal such as audition, competition, performance or simply for fun.!


Areas of Teaching:

Skill development which include pitch accuracy, range and breathing. Musicianship, style and stage presentation in preparation for audition and competitions. Repertoire study and interpretation. Live performing skill including achieving dynamics and microphone technique etc. Development of a healthier vocal habit which prevents sore throat and avoid damage to your vocal chords.

Qualification:

Bachelor of Fine Art, York University. Maj. Vocal. Eight years of private vocal training from top teachers in Toronto. Winner of numberous singing awards. Experienced performer,featured in major concert with audience up to 50 thousand people.

About Jane Lu

Canadian City Post Article about Singer Jane Lu.


Welcome to this website about excellent Hip Hop and R&B Singer Jane Lu, home of the popular singing sensation in the Chinese-Canadian community and overseas. Jane Lu is a multi-award winning singer who is currently working on her first debut album. The highly anticipated album will be a combination of many musical styles such as hip-hop, pop, rock and funk that will refresh the ear of listener. All the songs are written by Jane Lu herself and produced by Cory Noziglia from 199 Studios. The new album will include 11 tracks, all the tracks are carefully improved and professionally recorded. This full album is currently on its final stage, so please check back for any further updates regarding the release date. To get a sneak peek of Jane Lu's new album, please visit the video section and audio section for some of her CD demo.

November, 2006

Congratulation to Jane Lu for winning the Second-runner up in the First Minnan Singing Award in Fujian China. Jane Lu recently traveled all the way to Fujian China to represent Canada to compete in the First Minnan Singing Award. After 3 weeks of furious competition over 50 representatives from 4 countries, Jane Lu proudly took home the second-runner up.
